Common questions

What is a bridging loan?

A short-term loan used to buy a property quickly, before a normal mortgage is in place. It usually lasts a few months and is paid off in full when you refinance in step 3.

Why does refinancing give me my money back?

The bank lends against what the flat is worth now, not what you paid. Because it was bought below its value and refreshed, 80% of the new value is enough to pay off the bridging loan and return your own money.

What if the valuation comes in lower?

Then some of your money stays in the deal until the value rises. You still own the flat and still collect the rent. We only pick flats bought well below what similar ones have sold for, to give room for this.

Do I have to manage the tenant?

No. A letting agent finds the tenant and handles repairs, and that cost is already included in the £136 a month.
