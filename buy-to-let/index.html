Common questions

What is a bridging loan?

A short-term loan used to buy a property quickly, before a normal mortgage is in place. It usually lasts a few months and is paid off in full when you refinance in step 3.

Why does refinancing give me money back?

The bank lends against what the house is worth now, not what you paid. Because it was bought below its value and refurbished, 75% of the new value pays off the bridging loan and returns most of your own money.

What if the valuation comes in lower?

Then a little more of your money stays in the deal. You still own the house and still collect the rent. We only pick houses bought well below what similar ones have sold for, to give room for this.

Do I have to manage the tenant?

No. A letting agent finds the tenant and handles repairs, and that cost is already included before the £295 a month you keep.
