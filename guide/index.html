Questions people ask first

How much do I need to get started?

Anywhere from £50,000 onwards. We work with a range of budgets, from buy-to-lets to HMOs, and we'll find something that works for you. The specialist will go through the options for your budget on the call.

Do I own the property?

Yes. The property and the income are 100% yours. We charge a 2% acquisition fee and a 2% management fee, and never take a share of your property or profit.

Who will I speak to?

One of our HMO specialists. They work on our projects every day and can talk through real numbers, timelines and risks for your situation.

How involved do I need to be?

As much or as little as you like. You approve the house, the plan and the budget. We run everything day to day and keep you updated as it progresses.

Where do you buy?

Anywhere in the UK where the numbers work for an HMO. We look at demand from tenants, local HMO rules and Article 4, and what the house will be worth once converted. Our most recent completed projects are in Enfield, Harrow and Greenford.

What are the risks?

Build costs can run over, valuations can come in lower than expected, and rules on HMOs and renting change. Values and rents can fall as well as rise. We go through these openly before you commit to anything.
