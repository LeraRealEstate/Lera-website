Questions

What agents ask us.

What do I need to do after the introduction?

Nothing. We speak to your client, handle the deal and keep you posted. You just get paid when it completes.

When and how am I paid?

By bank transfer within 14 days of completion, based on Lera's fee for that deal. On a typical £25,000 fee, that's £3,000.

Is there a minimum number of introductions?

No. Introduce one client a year or one a week. There are no targets.

Do I have to join regular calls?

No. After your onboarding call, you can send intros whenever you like. Partners who want one can have a monthly 15-minute catch-up with our team.

Where do you work?

We take introductions from agents anywhere in the UK. Our investors buy mainly in and around London.

Will my client be poached?

No. We only work with your client on the purchase we're introduced for. Where they buy in your area, we offer you the lettings and management first.
