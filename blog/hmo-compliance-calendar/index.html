Running an HMO

The HMO compliance calendar: every certificate and check

Gas, electrics, EPCs, alarms, deposits and more: what's needed, how often, and what's coming next.

Lera Real Estate · Updated 31 October 2026 · 3 min read

Running an HMO means keeping on top of a long list of checks and certificates. Miss one and you risk fines, a lost licence or an invalid tenancy notice. Here's the full calendar.

WhatHow oftenNotes
Gas safety certificateEvery 12 monthsCopy to tenants within 28 days of the check
Electrical report (EICR)At least every 5 yearsCopy to tenants; urgent remedial work done promptly
Energy Performance CertificateValid for 10 yearsMinimum EPC E to let today; EPC C required by 1 October 2030, with a £10,000 cost cap
Smoke and CO alarmsTested at the start of every tenancySmoke alarm on every storey; CO alarm in any room with a fixed combustion appliance (not gas cookers)
Fire alarm and emergency lightingRegular servicingServiced to British Standards, with a logbook kept
Fire risk assessmentReviewed regularlyCovers the shared parts of the house
HMO licenceRenew every 5 years or lessLicence conditions kept up throughout
Tenancy depositsProtected within 30 daysIn a government-approved scheme, with the prescribed information
Right to Rent checksBefore each tenancy startsImmigration status checks on every adult tenant
Water safetyAssessed and reviewedA legionella risk assessment of the water system
Landlord databaseWhen it launchesRegistration on the new private rented sector database will be required before letting

Plus the HMO management regulations

Every HMO must also follow the HMO management regulations. The manager's contact details must be displayed in the house, fire escapes kept clear, shared areas, water, drainage, gas and electricity kept in good repair, and enough bins provided.

A sample year

WhenTask
WeeklyTest fire alarm and log it; check escape routes are clear
MonthlyTest emergency lighting (where fitted); inspect shared areas
Every 3 to 6 monthsRoom and property inspections; fire alarm servicing (often six-monthly)
Every 12 monthsGas safety check; full emergency lighting test; fire risk assessment review; insurance renewal; rent review if appropriate
Every 5 yearsEICR; HMO licence renewal (or sooner if the licence is shorter)
Every 10 yearsEPC renewal, or sooner if required
Every new tenancyRight to rent check; deposit protected within 30 days; required documents given; alarms checked on day one

Records to keep

  • every certificate and the date it was given to tenants
  • fire alarm and emergency lighting logs
  • inspection reports with photos
  • repair requests and when they were completed
  • licence, planning documents and insurance schedules

Good records protect you in disputes, help with renewals and reassure lenders and buyers. For the detail behind each check, read gas, electrical and alarm safety and EPC rules for HMOs.
