Running an HMO

Universal Credit tenants: the HMO market many investors overlook

The right tenants qualify for the full one-bedroom housing allowance, even when renting a single room.

Lera Real Estate · Updated 31 October 2026 · 3 min read

HMOs aren't only for young professionals. Many tenants pay their rent through Universal Credit, and the right tenants qualify for the full one-bedroom Local Housing Allowance (LHA) rate, even when they rent a single room in an HMO.

What the allowance is worth

Area (2026/27)One-bedroom LHA rate
Outer North London£1,147 a month
Inner North London£1,436 a month

For comparison, the room rent in our worked example is £1,042 a month. Rates are set for each area every April, and support is capped at the LHA rate for that area.

Who gets the one-bedroom rate in an HMO?

  • single tenants aged 35 and over
  • couples sharing one room
  • care leavers under 25
  • survivors of domestic abuse
  • people who've spent at least three months in a homeless hostel or refuge
  • some others, including certain disability benefit claimants, MAPPA-managed ex-offenders and survivors of modern slavery

Worth knowing

Most single Universal Credit claimants under 35 get the lower shared accommodation rate instead, so matching each room to the right tenant matters. Universal Credit is normally paid to the tenant, but a managed payment can be arranged so the housing costs go directly to the landlord, for example where there are arrears or the tenant needs support.

Housing costs and the shared accommodation rate

Most single people under 35 who rent privately get help with housing costs at the shared accommodation rate of Local Housing Allowance, which is set for a room in a shared house in each area. That makes a good HMO room the natural home for many younger claimants. Older claimants or those with certain exemptions may get the higher one-bedroom rate.

Practical tips for landlords

  • Check the current LHA rates for your area, as they set the realistic ceiling for rent support.
  • Explain clearly what's included in the rent. Bills-included rents are simple for tenants to budget.
  • Agree how rent will be paid from the start, and keep records.
  • Build good relationships with local support services and job centres.
  • Apply the same fair referencing to every applicant.

The law has changed

Since 1 May 2026, the Renters' Rights Act has made it unlawful in England to refuse someone just because they receive benefits. "No DSS" adverts and blanket bans are out. You can still check affordability, as long as you apply the same checks to everyone. See the Renters' Rights Act explained.

Two markets, one house

A well-designed en-suite HMO works for both working professionals and Universal Credit tenants. Having two strong tenant markets gives you more ways to fill a room quickly.
