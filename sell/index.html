Good questions.

Do you only buy tenanted property?

No. Our investors buy tenanted, empty and part-let property, in any condition.

Why is it free for me?

The investor who buys pays our fee. Nothing comes off your price.

How is the valuation worked out?

From recent sold prices of similar properties near you (HM Land Registry). We then confirm it with you, taking size, condition and rent into account.

How quickly can it sell?

Investors move faster than homebuyers because there's no chain to wait on. Once you accept an offer, the legal side usually takes a few weeks.

Am I tied in?

No. You only accept an offer you're happy with.
