Questions landlords ask

Will my tenants find out?

Only when you're ready. We value confidentially and arrange any viewings with you, around your tenants.

Will I get a fair price?

Investors price on rent and yield, so a well-let property can sell at full value. You only accept an offer you're happy with.

Am I tied in?

No. If we don't find a buyer in the time we agree, you're free to go, with nothing to pay.

Why is it free for me?

The investor who buys pays our fee, as part of the service we give them. Nothing comes out of your sale price.
