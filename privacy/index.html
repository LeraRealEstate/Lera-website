Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains how Lera Real Estate collects and uses your personal information when you visit lera-realestate.com, download one of our guides, book a call, fill in one of our forms (including forms on Facebook and Instagram), or contact us.

Who we are

Lera Real Estate is a trading name of AS London 4 Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 16419301). We are the controller of your personal information. You can contact us at info@lera-realestate.com.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us: your name, email address and phone number.
  • Information about what you are looking for, such as the type of property, your budget, your timeline and the areas you are interested in.
  • Details of any call you book with us and what we discuss.
  • Basic technical information when you use our website, such as the page you came from and advertising tags (for example, which ad you clicked).

How we use it

  • To send you the guide or information you asked for.
  • To contact you by phone, WhatsApp or email about property investments and deals that match what you are looking for.
  • To arrange and hold calls and meetings with you.
  • To improve our website and measure how well our advertising works.

We rely on your consent when you submit a form, and on our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and running our business. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

Who we share it with

We never sell your information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, such as our email, calendar, spreadsheet and advertising platforms (for example Google, MailerLite and Meta), and only so they can provide those services to us. If you choose to go ahead with a property, we may share relevant details with the solicitors, brokers and other professionals involved, with your agreement.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we are in touch about property investments, and for up to two years after our last contact, unless you ask us to delete it sooner or we must keep it longer by law.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to how we use it, or ask us to stop contacting you. Just email info@lera-realestate.com or reply "stop" to any message. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

Our website uses a small number of cookies and similar tools, including the Meta pixel, to understand visits and measure our advertising. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
